## 0. Summary in ten lines

1. The best recent rebrands are **evolutions that make one asset do more**: Walmart's spark, Lamborghini's bull,
   OpenAI's blossom and the 2026 Xbox sphere all kept their geometry and changed only finish, type or freedom of use.
2. Full replacements work when there was **no equity to lose** (Decathlon had no logo at all) or when the old
   name no longer described the company (Mozilla wanted to be seen beyond Firefox). AID has little logo equity, so
   it has room to choose.
3. Marks now earn meaning in four ways: **geometry** (the shape says one thing), **origin** (a true and checkable
   story), **behaviour** (it moves in ways that mean something) and **modularity** (parts work alone at 16 px).
4. In the agentic era a mark must also be an **agent avatar with states**, such as listening, thinking,
   responding and done. Meta's AI ring, OpenAI's "Point" and Claude's pulsing spark all do this.
5. The type lesson is **one family, used strictly**. Polestar uses one typeface at one size and one weight.
   OpenAI built one sans to replace many. Rivian drew its numerals from its own headlights.
6. Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk, Manrope, DM Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, Poppins, Outfit and Sora now read as
   **"unchosen defaults"**. Anthropic's own guidance names Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato and Space Grotesk.
7. Colour restraint means **a monochrome base plus one owned hue**, with status colours kept separate and reserved
   (Polestar, Anduril, GOV.UK, Carbon, Linear).
8. Defence tech has its own default, which one critic calls **"Defence Dun"**: beige, cream or black with a safe
   grotesk, so that Anduril and Helsing "could swap… with little effort". AID Defence must be restrained without
   looking the same as everyone else.
9. Two brands that share a name work best when they share **one structural asset** and differ in colour, voice
   and treatment (Swiggy → Instamart, Tata → TATA.ev). Saab's griffin dispute shows that a shared mark needs
   clear ownership and rules.
10. Recommended logo direction: **evolve the current mark** (direction B, §7.3), tested against a wordmark-led
    replacement (C). Recommended fonts to test first: **Geologica**, **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next**, **Anek
    Latin/Devanagari**, with **IBM Plex** as the safe fallback (§4.5).

### 1.2 Patterns across the cases

| Pattern | Evidence | What it means for AID |
|---|---|---|
| **Keep the geometry, change the finish** | Xbox 2026 (same vector outline), Walmart (spark kept), OpenAI (blossom kept), Lamborghini (bull kept) | Only worth it if there is recognition to protect. AID's mark has little public recognition, so the shape is open. |
| **Free the symbol** | Lamborghini bull alone on digital; Polestar star out of its square; Walmart spark as a standalone asset | AID's symbol must work alone at 16 px, without the wordmark or colours. |
| **Unify type** | OpenAI Sans replaces many fonts; Polestar uses one; Mozilla's three faces come from one pixel logic | The user's "one Google Fonts sans for both brands" matches best practice. |
| **Motion carries meaning** | Mozilla flag → dinosaur; Meta ring in/out; OpenAI Point; Perplexity spinning cursor | Plan the mark's states before you finalise its shape. |
| **Warmth from truth, not decoration** | Mistral's cat, Walmart's hat, Bosch's armature, Rivian's headlight zero | AID's story must come from something true about AID (positioning, NavIC, three layers built in-house). |
| **Category escape** | Claude avoids blue; Innomotics picks lime after an audit; Mistral goes retro; Helsing uses landscapes | Audit IoT and defence competitors' colours before choosing (reports 07–09 feed this). |

## 2. Logo storytelling

### 2.1 Four ways a mark earns meaning

| Route | How it works | Best examples | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Geometry** | The shape says one thing at a glance (an orbit, a footnote, a wing) | Decathlon Orbit; Perplexity asterisk; Polestar star | Generic shapes (triangles, orbits, sparkles) collide with others: Decathlon was compared to Asics |
| **Origin** | A true story people can check (archive, product, place) | Walmart's trucker hat; Bosch armature; Mistral's dataset cat; Mozilla's Shepard Fairey dinosaur | Made-up stories read as post-rationalised. If the story needs a paragraph, it isn't working. |
| **Behaviour** | The mark moves in a way that means something, with states | Meta AI ring (in = listening, out = responding); OpenAI Point; Claude spark pulsing while it works; Mozilla cursor blink | Motion for its own sake becomes noise. Every state must map to a system state. |
| **Modularity** | Parts work alone: symbol, wordmark, a single detail, a pattern | Lamborghini bull without shield; Anthropic's slash; Mozilla's pixel grid; Jio banners | Too many parts break consistency. Define which parts may stand alone. |

The strongest marks combine **two** routes. Perplexity uses geometry and behaviour: it is a footnote that spins
like a cursor. Mozilla uses origin and behaviour: a flag that becomes the dinosaur. Bosch uses geometry and
origin: it is a drawing of the product.

### 2.2 Building a mark for the agentic era

Marks now live as **agent avatars, app icons, favicons, chat bubbles, device LEDs and status indicators**, not
only on websites. A checklist drawn from the cases above:

1. **Size ladder.** Design at 16, 24, 32, 48 px and on a 1024 px app icon. Below 24 px the mark should drop to its
   simplest form, with no thin strokes or internal detail. Lamborghini, Polestar and Walmart all freed the symbol
   for this reason.
2. **One-colour first.** It must work in a single colour on light, dark and night-mode (red-on-black) surfaces, as
   a laser etch and as an embossed enclosure mark. Colour is a layer, not the idea.
3. **States, not just animation.** Define idle, listening/receiving, working/thinking, responding/sending, done
   and alert. Meta delivered "16 voice state animations" as Lottie files ([Animade](https://archive.animade.tv/work/meta-ai-ring)).
   Claude's spark pulses while it works [fetched DesignRush]. OpenAI's Point "pulses, expands and connects"
   ([D&AD](https://www.dandad.org/work/d-ad-awards-archive/openai-brand-film)).
4. **Reduced-motion version.** Every state needs a static equivalent, such as a change of shape or fill rather
   than movement (an AA requirement already in CLAUDE.md).
5. **Avoid AI-era clichés.** These are four-point sparkles (Gemini, and many others), gradient orbs, purple-blue
   glows, neural meshes and "brain + circuit". Claude's mark is admired because it is irregular and warm, not
   because it is a sparkle.
6. **The mark should describe behaviour.** Perplexity's footnote describes citing. For AID the true behaviours
   are **sensing → connecting → deciding** (IoT) and **position → bearing → coordination** (Defence; see
   `docs/brand/STRATEGY-CONTRAST.md`).

### 2.3 Two brands that share a name

AID IoT and AID Defence are separate identities on a shared core, and each must never mention the other
(STRATEGY-CONTRAST). They still share the word "AID", so they need a clear model:

| Model | Example | How it works | Fit for AID |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Same symbol, different colour and name** | Swiggy S-Pin kept by Instamart, with a new blue [fetched] | The symbol links; colour and name separate | Good if leadership accepts a visible link between the brands |
| **Same name, suffix and own colour** | TATA.ev (".ev" + Evo Teal) [search] | The parent name stays; the suffix and colour define the sub-brand | Good fit: "AID IoT" / "AID Defence" already work as suffixes |
| **Same skeleton, different treatment** | Siemens / Siemens Healthineers (shared wordmark, Healthineers adds orange and its own type) [search]; Google pulling Gemini *into* its colours [fetched] | One drawing, different finish, weight, colour and motion | **Best fit.** The wordmark skeleton is shared; each brand has its own finish |
| **Separate symbols, shared wordmark only** | Siemens carve-out Innomotics went fully separate (lime, own face) [fetched] | Maximum separation | Only if Defence must be fully deniable |
| **Governance warning** | Saab griffin needed approval from three units [search] | Shared marks create shared vetoes | Record in DECISIONS: the parent (Inordinate) owns the "AID" marks; each brand owns only its treatment |

**Recommendation:** use the same skeleton with a different treatment. Draw one "AID" wordmark and one symbol
skeleton. AID IoT renders them open, rounded, warm and flowing. AID Defence renders them closed, sharper, darker
and step-wise. If you cover the logo, a reader should still know which brand they are looking at, because
colour, type treatment and imagery differ.

### 3.2 Keeping status colours separate from brand colour

- **GOV.UK** reserves its focus colour: "Only use this colour to indicate which element is focused on"
  (#ffdd00). Red is only for errors and green only for success ([GOV.UK colour](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/colour/)) [fetched].
- **IBM Carbon**: "Alert colors are used to reflect status": red for danger, orange for serious, yellow for
  warning and green for success. These are kept apart from the categorical data-visualisation palettes, and
  gradients "should not be used to represent any meaningful progression"
  ([Carbon](https://carbondesignsystem.com/data-visualization/color-palettes/)) [fetched].
- **Astro UXDS** (space and defence consoles) has six status levels: critical, serious, caution, normal, standby
  and off. Each has its own colour *and* its own shape, so that status never depends on colour alone. [background:
  the page would not render for fetching; verify at astrouxds.com]
- **Linear** keeps its accent as one of three theme inputs and limits how far it spreads [fetched].

**Rules for AID**
1. Neutrals plus **one owned hue per brand**. IoT gets a warmer, lighter one. Defence gets a darker, quieter one
   that is not beige, cream or black-only.
2. **Status colours are a separate, shared token set.** They are never used for decoration and never the same
   value as either brand hue. Each status has an icon or shape too.
3. **Reserve one colour for focus**, as GOV.UK does.
4. **Data-visualisation palettes are separate** from both brand and status colours.
5. Night mode for Lighthouse is its own palette (red/amber on black), not an inverted brand palette.

### 4.3 What AID needs from the family

- Two personalities from **one family**: IoT (more air, lighter, warm) and Defence (tighter, firmer, labels and
  codes). The best tool for this is a **variable axis** such as width, sharpness or flare, not two fonts.
- **Tabular figures** (`tnum`) and ideally a **slashed or dotted zero** (`zero`) for specs, coordinates and
  telemetry. Rivian built a monospace variant for exactly this.
- Clear letter shapes (I/l/1, O/0) for senior readers and night-mode consoles.
- A **matching mono** if possible, for code, IDs and data labels.
- **Devanagari** later: either inside the family or in a matched companion family.

## 6. Do / don't against AI slop

**Do**
- Choose a font for a reason you can write in one sentence, such as "sharpness axis gives Defence its own
  treatment".
- Use one owned hue per brand, chosen after a competitor colour audit (Innomotics).
- Use tabular figures and a slashed zero for every number that changes or gets compared.
- Use real product photography or believable, specific AI imagery, with a sidecar prompt file (CLAUDE.md rule).
- Use motion only where it shows a state or a mechanism, and design a reduced-motion state for each.
- Write copy with a number or a named outcome in the first two lines.
- Make one memorable detail per brand (Anthropic's slash, Rivian's zero).
- Use status colours only for status, each with a shape or icon.

**Don't**
- Use Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk, Manrope, DM Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, Poppins, Outfit, Sora or Montserrat.
- Use indigo-to-purple gradients, gradient text, cyan on dark, radial glows behind content, or neon dark mode.
- Use sparkles, gradient orbs, neural meshes, holographic cities, glowing globes or "brain + circuit" marks.
- Use beige/cream "tasteful default" palettes, or Defence Dun (beige, cream, black + Helvetica).
- Use bento grids, three identical cards in a row, icon tiles above headings, badge pills above hero headlines,
  or decorative grid backgrounds.
- Use pulsing dots that signal nothing, decorative blinking cursors, marquees or bouncy easing.
- Use "world-class", "seamless", "revolutionary", "AI-powered", "leverage", or em-dashes in every sentence.
- Rely on a texture gimmick (pixels, dots, glitch) as the core idea. Nothing moved away from its pixel wordmark
  after five years.
- Flatten a mark by subtraction and call it modern (The Drive on Lamborghini).

### 7.2 Logo: three directions

The current mark has three pill strokes forming a rounded triangle that reads as an "A" without a crossbar,
plus three dots at the corners. It uses three colours (green, blue, orange) arranged so that each dot differs
from its neighbouring strokes. It has no stated story, and three colours conflict with the "too many colours"
complaint.

**A useful true story already exists in the product: triangulation.** A position fix needs at least three
references, which is how NavIC/GNSS positioning works for NAVIC and Lighthouse. Three nodes joined by three
links is also an honest picture of what AID builds: device, connection and platform, all in-house. Each
direction below uses or rejects this story differently.

| | **A. Keep and give it a story** | **B. Evolve (recommended)** | **C. Replace** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | Same drawing. Name the story: "three nodes, one fix", for sense–connect–decide and for triangulation | Keep the "A + three nodes" skeleton but redraw as **one continuous monoline** with three nodes; one colour per brand; the crossbar-less A stays | Wordmark-led: a custom-drawn "AID" from the chosen family with **one detail** (for example, the counter of the D as a bearing notch, or the dot as a node), plus a small glyph for icon use |
| IoT treatment | Keep three colours (or reduce to one) | Rounded joins, open path; motion = a signal travelling node → node → node (sense → connect → decide) | Warm hue; rounded detail; flowing reveal |
| Defence treatment | Same mark in one dark colour | Same skeleton, **closed and sharper** (like Geologica SHRP 100); nodes become bearing ticks; motion = step-wise "fix acquired" | Dark neutral + quiet accent; sharp detail; step reveal |
| 16 px / avatar | Three dots read; pill strokes blur | Three dots + one line; clear states (idle dot, travelling dot, all three lit = done, one node red = alert) | Glyph only; needs to be drawn for the size |
| Pros | Fastest; keeps continuity; story is true | Keeps what little recognition exists, fixes colour overload, gives behaviour and a two-brand system from one drawing | Cleanest for the agentic era; no colour baggage; most ownable if the detail is strong |
| Cons / risks | Triangle-of-pills looks generic and close to multi-colour tech marks; three colours break restraint; the story feels added later | Needs careful drawing so it doesn't become just another triangle (check Delta, Adidas, Arduino-style marks) | Loses existing mark; highest craft burden; wordmarks are harder to animate as states |
| Precedent | Walmart spark, Xbox (finish change) | OpenAI blossom refinement, Lamborghini bull freed, Mozilla flag ↔ dinosaur behaviour | Anthropic slash wordmark, Polestar, Decathlon (new symbol) |

**Recommendation:** develop **B**, with **C** as the comparison route in the same review. Keep **A** only if
leadership needs continuity for existing hardware or packaging. Test all of them at 16/24/32 px, as a
one-colour laser etch, in night mode, as a five-state Lottie avatar, and with the covered-logo test from
STRATEGY-CONTRAST. Check trademark clearance for triangle and "A" marks in Classes 9, 38, 42 and 13 before
finalising.

### 7.3 Font recommendation

1. **Test Geologica, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Anek Latin/Devanagari** in the §4.5 protocol.
   - Geologica wins if the "one family, two treatments" story (sharpness axis) matters most.

   - Anek wins if Indian design origin and built-in Devanagari matter most.
2. **IBM Plex** is the fallback for product UI (Lighthouse, Signal), because it is the only complete
