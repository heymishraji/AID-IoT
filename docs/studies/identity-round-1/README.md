## What is here
- `logo-*.svg`: the six route symbols as drawn in Figma (120 × 120 grid). Drafts for review, not final marks.
  A = current mark kept; B = one line through three nodes (recommended); C = wordmark-led A with a node.
  AID IoT versions are ink on paper; AID Defence versions are bone for dark grounds.
- `colour-routes.json` + `palette.py`: the six colour routes (brand scale 100/300/500/700/900) and neutrals, built
  in OKLCH and gamut-mapped; `palette.py` prints WCAG contrast. AID IoT: Copper (lean), Indigo, Jamun.
  AID Defence: Slate & Brass (lean), Graphite & Glacier, Basalt & Bone.
- `reference-urls.tsv`: the 51 reference pages captured for board 01 (screenshots live only in Figma; they are
  third-party pages and are not stored in the repo). Six refused automated capture: Epirus, Havoc AI, Anduril,
  Framework, Nothing, Geist's Anthropic case study.
- Generated mood images and their prompts: `assets/imagery/generated/`.
