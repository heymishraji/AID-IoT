Decide

Claude's recommendation

Anek Latin, if it passes your eye test at 12px and in the night console. It is the only candidate that gives AID one matching design in Latin and Devanagari from an Indian foundry. Its width axis also lets Lighthouse tables and datasheets run compact without a second font.

If the small print or the callsigns feel harder to read than in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, choose Atkinson instead: legibility in the field matters more than the Devanagari match, which can be solved with a paired face later.

  1. Switch faces with keys 1–4 and read the dashboard, the night console and the small print in each.
  2. Ignore Geologica's charm in the big headline if it slows you down in the tables: the system spends most of its life in tables.
