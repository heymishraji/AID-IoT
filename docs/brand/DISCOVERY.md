# Discovery answers

Source: product/design lead (brand owner), 2026-10-27. Gaps marked **[Leadership]** await the CEO/founders.
Question numbers follow the questionnaire in `docs/research/01-brand-system-anatomy.md` §2.

## Structure
- Legal parent: **Inordinate Industrial Innovation Private Limited**.
- Two separate brands under it: **AID IoT** and **AID Defence** (Indian spelling, "Defence").
- Each brand has its own visuals, imagery and language, because the audiences barely overlap. People outside
  defence should not need to meet AID Defence.
- There should be one brand umbrella for the identity, and one font family across the parent and both brands.
  An accent face may differ per brand only where it earns its place.
- Must be portable: each brand can be lifted, complete, into its own repository.

## Personality and voice (Q19–22)
- **AID IoT:** a solver. It understands the problem statement correctly before proposing anything. It is
  empathetic, clear and compassionate, innovative, and agile: it adapts and customises to the need. Open to
  learn, accept and build.
- **AID Defence:** mature, very clear, no nonsense, on point. It explains technical products so that senior,
  non-technical defence staff understand them. It is private and discreet: it never leads with anything
  sensitive, shows a glimpse of the problem and the approach, and invites a private conversation.
  Sliders: formal, conversational, reserved and bold; an established challenger that takes on any requirement.
- **Admired:** Apple (simplicity, clarity, sharp product positioning); Siemens and Samsung (innovative, and
  structured clearly enough that non-technical people understand the problem and solution).
- **Disliked:** AI slop; noise and unnecessary information; jargon; too many colours; anything unprofessional
  or inconsistent.
- Always/never words (Q22): later.

## Identity assets (Q23–26)
- **Logo:** simple and recreatable (also exists in Figma). It has no fixed meaning yet. Keep it for now, but
  evolution or full replacement is open if a stronger mark and story emerge for the agentic era.
  References for the scale of change: Xbox, Lamborghini, Mozilla, Mistral, Walmart (subtle), Decathlon.
- Fonts, colours, templates and photo library: none exist; all are new. Type must be sans serif, very clear,
  free of AI-slop clichés, and from Google Fonts (consistency and performance).
- No existing asset has customer recognition, so there is nothing to preserve.
- Physical products have no constraints. Enclosures, CMF, labels and logo placement will be designed later
  (NAVIC first), after research.

## Operations and governance (Q31–34)
- Owner: the product/design lead is the sole owner of brand and design releases, requirements and versioning.
- Production is all in-house; no agencies.
- **Tools:** Google Slides, Figma (paid plan; Figma MCP connected), Canva, Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Claude Code.
- **AI content:** no limit. AI imagery is preferred because real sites and kit cannot be shown for privacy and
  sensitivity reasons. Images come from ChatGPT image generation and Figma. Mood boards go in Figma.
