# AID IoT vs AID Defence — strategy at a glance (v0 draft)

_Status: draft v0, 2026-10-27. One page for designers and agents. Sources: `aid-iot/STRATEGY.md`,
`aid-iot/VOICE.md`, `aid-defence/STRATEGY.md`, `aid-defence/VOICE.md`, `PARENT.md`. Visual rows are
directional only: no colour values or fonts yet (those come in each brand's `DESIGN.md`)._

**Shared by both:** one Google Fonts sans family; shared grid, spacing and component foundations; British
English; plain language, outcome or problem first; AA accessibility; no claims without proof; legal parent
Inordinate Industrial Innovation Private Limited (legal line only, see `PARENT.md`).

| Dimension | AID IoT | AID Defence |
|---|---|---|
| **Core idea (umbrella)** | Connected systems that fit your problem — because we understand it first. | Hard requirements, answered clearly and discreetly. |
| **Lead positioning (draft)** | The problem-first partner that builds every layer | The established challenger that takes on any requirement |
| **Primary audience (draft)** | Non-IT executives running physical operations; engineering evaluators one level down | Senior, non-technical officers; technical evaluators in private |
| **Promise** | Understood first. Built to fit. | Any requirement taken seriously. Every answer clear. Everything discreet. |
| **Personality** | Empathetic · Clear · Inventive · Adaptable | Mature · Clear · Discreet · Resolute |
| **Archetype (optional)** | Caregiver-Creator | Sage-Guardian |
| **Voice in one line** | A capable engineer who listens first, then explains the fix in plain words. | A senior engineer briefing a senior officer: calm, exact, brief. |
| **Formal ↔ Casual** | 3 · professional, conversational | 2 · formal yet conversational |
| **Technical ↔ Plain** | 4 · plain first, detail one level down | 4 · plain in public, precision in private |
| **Reserved ↔ Bold** | 3 · confident, modest about the unproven | 2 / 4 · reserved in disclosure, bold in commitment |
| **Serious ↔ Warm** | 4 · warm, kind, never cute | 2 · serious and courteous |
| **Established ↔ Challenger** | 3.5–4 · capable newcomer | 3 · established manner, challenger stance |
| **Sentence length** | 12–18 words, max 25 | 10–16 words, max 22 |
| **Disclosure** | Open: show the product, the mechanism and the specs | Three tiers: public glimpse → on request → briefing only |
| **Proof style** | Customer outcomes with numbers, how-it-works, protocols, case stories [all pending] | Certifications, test evidence, trials, indigenous content, lifecycle support — shown at the right tier [all pending] |
| **Primary call to action** | "Tell us the problem" | "Request a private briefing" |
| **Imagery stance** | Human and in context: the problem, the place, the system working. AI-generated, warm, believable; mechanism over atmosphere. | Restrained renders: product or abstract system on neutral ground; terrain without identifiable places; people absent or obscured. Never real kit, units, combat or weapons-as-hero. |
| **Colour stance** | Warmer and lighter; a small, purposeful set; colour explains (state, flow), never decorates | Darker and quieter; near-neutral with one restrained accent; avoid the monochrome "Defence Dun" sameness (research 01 §6.4) |
| **Typography stance** | Same family; more air, lighter weights, generous spacing | Same family; tighter, firmer; labels and codes; tabular figures for specs |
| **Motion stance** | Flowing: shows data moving from device to decision; explains a mechanism | Precise and step-wise, slower; reveals in sequence, like a briefing |
| **Graphic idea (to explore)** | Signal flow: problem → device → insight | Navigation: position, bearing, coordination |
| **Clichés to avoid** | Navy + orange glow, glowing meshes, holographic cities, bento, "engineer with tablet" | Camouflage, crosshairs, black-and-red threat, fake HUDs, tricolour wash, soldier at sunset |
| **Words to avoid (sample)** | revolutionary, seamless, leverage, AI-powered | lethal, dominate, battle-proven, unmatched, warfighter |
| **Social stance** | Real problems and real builds; warm, human | Company news and considered thinking; no specs, no deployments |
| **Never** | Mentions AID Defence | Mentions AID IoT; glorifies force |

## How to use this page
- **Designers:** if a design would sit equally well on both brands, it belongs to the shared foundation —
  or it is not yet doing its job for either brand.
- **Agents:** pick the column first. Then read that brand's `VOICE.md` and run its self-check.
- **Test of difference:** cover the logo. A reader should still know which brand they are looking at.

## Open questions for leadership
1. Do the two core ideas and promises feel true and different enough?
2. Is the IoT brand's openness (specs public) compatible with any customer confidentiality?
3. Approve the slider contrast — especially serious ↔ warm (4 vs 2).
4. Any channel where the brands must appear together (e.g. investor deck, joint tender)?
