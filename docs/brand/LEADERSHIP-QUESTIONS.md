## 9. Legal, naming and export control

Names end up in every logo, label and document, so they are cheap to fix now and expensive later.

**The NAVIC name needs a decision.** NavIC is the official name of ISRO's Indian satellite navigation system. A navigation product called NAVIC may be read as ISRO-affiliated, may be refused as a trademark, and will never rank above ISRO in search. Our recommended option is to give the product line its own name and describe it as "NavIC-enabled".

| # | Question | Why we ask | Your answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 9.2 | Which names are registered or applied for, and in which classes and countries? (AID, AID IoT, AID Defence, NAVIC, Lighthouse, Cortex, others.) | Tells us which names are safe to build on |  |
| 9.3 | Which domains and social handles do we own for each brand? | Decides web addresses and handles |  |
