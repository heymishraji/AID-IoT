The problem

Industrial data is everywhere — and nowhere.

The data exists; it just never reaches the people who decide.

Readings stay in the panel

Machines log locally; nobody sees it until someone walks over.

One radio, one point of failure

When the link drops, the data is gone — not delayed.

Nothing decides on-site

Alerts and simple rules wait on a round-trip to a server.

Boxes that lock you in

Off-the-shelf gateways bring their own cloud and pricing.
