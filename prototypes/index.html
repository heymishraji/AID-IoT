AID-IoT · website rebuild · Phase 0

Three directions, as working prototypes

Each is a scrollable homepage with real motion, real AID-IoT product imagery and realistic operational data. Judge the idea, the world and the voice — not pixel polish. Open each on desktop and on your phone (the server is on your local network too).

Direction A

Open the device

The real product opens on scroll into the six layers AID-IoT makes. Three.js, scroll-scrubbed. Signal trace as the page spine.Type: Cabinet Grotesk · Switzer · Martian Mono (ITF + free) Direction B

Panel room

A live mimic diagram of a plant line as the hero; scroll walks you from sensor to decision; hover nodes for the device and its module.Type: Khand · Ranade · Martian Mono (ITF + free) Direction C

Field log

Documentary photography with parallax chapters; the paper register dissolves into the Signal record on a pinned scroll.Type: Zodiak · Author · Martian Mono (ITF + free)

What is shared across all three

  • Five-branch navigation (Solutions · Products · Software · Services · Company) + Store · Log in · Talk to an engineer.
  • Story spine Connect · Communicate · Automate · Scale; the platform is called Signal.
  • Real AID-IoT product photos; documentary photos from Pexels as stand-ins for a shoot (see assets/img/CREDITS.md).
  • Reduced-motion and mobile behaviours are implemented, not promised.
