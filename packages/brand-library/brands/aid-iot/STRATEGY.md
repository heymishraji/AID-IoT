## 2. Purpose (draft options — pick one)
Purpose is why we exist beyond revenue. One sentence. Built on the owner's "solver" description.
- **A. Understanding first.** "We exist to understand a real problem properly, then build the connected
  system that solves it."
- **B. Technology that fits.** "We exist to make connected technology fit the work, not the other way round."
- **C. Fewer blind spots.** "We exist to help people see and run their physical operations with less guesswork."

Recommendation: **A**. It is the only one that carries the owner's core idea (understand before proposing)
and it is hard for a product-catalogue competitor to claim. B is a good line for copy. C is generic.

## 8. Brand personality — the empathetic solver
The owner's words: a solver that understands the problem first; empathetic, clear and compassionate,
innovative, agile; open to learn, accept and build. Expanded into four traits:

| Trait | What it means | This | Not that |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Empathetic** | We take the customer's problem as seriously as they do. We ask before we answer. | Curious, attentive, patient | Presumptuous, salesy, "we know best" |
| **Clear** | We explain things so a non-technical executive gets it in one read. | Plain, specific, honest about limits | Vague, jargon-heavy, or dumbed down |
| **Inventive** | We look for the better way, and we build it. | Practical ingenuity that solves the stated problem | Novelty for its own sake, buzzwords, "AI-powered everything" |
| **Adaptable** | We change the product to fit the need, and we learn as we go. | Flexible, open, quick to adjust | Shapeless, "we do anything", no point of view |

Archetype (optional, for prompting): **the Caregiver-Creator** — helps first, then makes. Not the Hero
(rescues the customer) and not the Magician (mystifies the technology).

## 11. What AID IoT never does
- Claims a customer, number or certification that is not verified and cleared.
- Leads with technology before the problem.
- Mentions or links to the parent company's other brand.
- Uses category clichés: glowing meshes, holographic cities, navy-and-orange glow, stock "engineer with tablet".
